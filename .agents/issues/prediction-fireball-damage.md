what: online, the client's own prediction can hurt its own player. PredictLocalPlayer moves the local player through the shared movement code; when it touches another player's fireball replica, HandleCollision calls DamageEntity in the client's world and takes 25 health the server never took (the server's fireball missed). The next snapshot puts the health back, so it shows as a 50 ms dip. TestReplicasMatchTheServer catches it as "parity on Old Arena: ... wrong health 1" (the watcher, frame 474: snapshot says 100, client shows 75 after its tick, corrected on the next snapshot).
reproduce: on main the bots' fights happen not to cross the watcher. Branch agent/maps-relayout-arena (the Old Arena relayout, which only moves where bots fight) fails server_tests every run. To see it, print DamageEntity calls on players with their World pointer: one comes from the client's world, source a FireBall, right between the frames where the health differs.
fix: keep predicted movement from dealing or taking hits: skip the damage in HandleCollision while the client predicts (a flag on app_state set around PredictLocalPlayer), or give the replica world no player-versus-fireball collision rule. Then land agent/maps-relayout-arena (Old Arena with stands, hills, stairs and jumpables).
