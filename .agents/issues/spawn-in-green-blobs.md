what: offline on Old Arena, a player standing still at the spawn (as one does behind the Play screen at launch) is shelled within seconds by a Bilecaller Toad it cannot see, and stands in the bile puddles it leaves (the "green blobs"); in one run it died twice in 25 s. Root cause, measured in the running client: at frame 240 (8 s; the client runs 1/30 s frames) the player is at 74 health with three toad hazards (type 9, kind 3, ability 0) at 0 and 41 units, and no monster within 200 units. The toad's Bile Barrage reaches 380 units (AggroRange 400), but the window shows about 340 units above and below the player, so the toad fires from off screen. Each shell is telegraphed on the ground for 1 s, so a moving player can dodge it; a player at the spawn is not moving.
reproduce: set GAME_SERVER=offline, start build\win32_app.exe from build\, leave the Play screen open and do not move; health drops within 8 s. (A pure SimulateTick probe at 1/60 s does not show it on this seed: run it at 1/30 s like the client.)
fix: one of: keep Bile Barrage's MaxRange (and AggroRange) under the visible half-height, about 300; or keep monsters from targeting a player for the first few seconds after a spawn or respawn; or spawn monsters, and toads in particular, farther from player spawn points.
