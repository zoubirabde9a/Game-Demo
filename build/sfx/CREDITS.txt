All from Kenney (www.kenney.nl), Creative Commons Zero (CC0): free for
personal, educational and commercial use, no credit required.

To swap one: put a WAV in the format above at build/sfx/<name>.wav (the
name is the last column of SoundEffects in code/client/sounds/sounds.cpp).
Delete it to go back to the sound made in code.
